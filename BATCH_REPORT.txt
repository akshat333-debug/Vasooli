  Headline metric is recovery per attempt, because the retry budget is
  the scarce resource - three attempts, then the subscription halts.
  'Recoverable halted' is what that costs in customers: a live mandate,
  a recoverable failure, an amount inside both caps, and every attempt
  burned anyway. Each one is a subscription Razorpay marks halted. The
  sequencer's preserved attempts are what keep these alive next cycle.

BASIS - raw and compliance-adjusted, side by side
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  baseline  raw Rs 57,571.00   adjusted Rs 57,571.00   above the cap Rs 0.00
  sequencer raw Rs 58,875.00   adjusted Rs 58,875.00   above the cap Rs 0.00

  These now coincide, and that is the finding. An earlier version of
  this engine credited the baseline with above-cap recoveries and the
  report subtracted them afterwards - which meant the compliance
  headline was correcting a simulator error, not measuring a behaviour.
  Three independent layers refuse an above-cap debit now: the stopping
  rule declines it, the money-side breaker refuses it at the boundary,
  and the world declines it on presentation because RBI requires AFA.
  So there is one basis, no adjustment to argue about, and the number
  above stands on its own.
